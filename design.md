# ComfartZone — Visual Design and Interaction System

**Status:** Revised approved product direction; implementation details remain subject to architecture review.  
**Companions:** `docs/product-spec.md`, `docs/architecture.md`, `docs/test-plan.md`, `AGENTS.md`.  
**Visual references:** `references/Reference.png` and `references/Reference1.png`.  
**Design goal:** At least 80% visual fidelity to the approved references, judged by side-by-side mobile and desktop screenshots. Cozy, sharp, slightly ridiculous, and unmistakably a real community—not generic SaaS or a childish game.

**Specification precedence:** `docs/product-spec.md` governs product behavior and privacy; this document governs visual and interaction design. Flag contradictions for review instead of silently overriding either document.

## 1. Art direction

Blend warm, expressive, slightly cartoonish personality with a clean, dense, readable social feed. Posts—not illustrations—are the main attraction.

**Visual adjectives:** warm, witty, compact, friendly, mischievous, tactile, readable.  
**Avoid:** glassmorphism, neon, gradient-heavy hero blocks, excessive whitespace, corporate stock art, giant typography, fake metrics, pill-everything, AI-dashboard styling.

### Brand

- Header **left:** a recognizable **CZ-only** logo linking home. Do not display the full wordmark or “The zone” in the header.
- Header **right:** one profile/avatar control, showing the user's selected avatar or username initial; guests see a sign-in affordance. Do not duplicate Profile in the bottom navigation.
- Provide an original small grumpy potato-like mascot, with dark outlines and expressive eyes, for occasional empty, success/error and 404 states—not every card. Lightweight SVG preferred.
- Users receive a unique autogenerated username, editable during signup and changeable no more than once per rolling 30 days. They can choose from provided avatars. No real-name requirement, follower counts, karma or verification badges.

### Voice and copy

- Brief, conversational, dryly funny, human.
- Functional controls stay clear: `Report`, `Delete`, `Cancel`, `Sort`, `Reply`.
- Composer prompt: **“What ruined your day today?”**; posting CTA: **“Spill it”**.
- Avoid corporate welcomes, generic safe-space slogans, forced hustle/productivity/laziness jokes and developer-facing prototype language.
- Humor belongs in prompts and occasional feedback, not every control. Errors explain what happened and what to do next. Never mock users reporting harm.

### Shape, spacing, typography and icons

- Spacing: 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 32 px. Cards generally 12–16 px internal padding, compact 8–12 px gaps.
- Card radius 14–16 px, dialog 18–20 px, input 10–12 px; rounded pills only where useful.
- Warm subtle 1 px borders; restrained shadows for floating surfaces.
- Primary controls ~40–44 px high; touch targets at least 44×44 px where practical.
- Body 15–16 px, line height 1.45–1.6; metadata 12–13 px. No oversized feed headings.
- System sans stack initially; optional lightweight display font if it improves reference fidelity. Unicode rendering must be reliable.
- Lucide outline icons, consistent 1.8–2 px strokes, usually 18–20 px; accessible labels for standalone icons. Emojis never the sole functional label.

## 3. Responsive shell and navigation

### Mobile: 320–767 px

- Header 52–60 px: CZ logo left; one avatar/sign-in action right. No redundant wordmark, “The zone”, or second profile destination.
- Full-width feed with 12–16 px gutters. Compact composer immediately followed by compact feed controls and posts.
- **No duplicate post-type tabs** when bottom navigation provides post-type destinations. The bottom bar should prioritize meaningful user activities, not force every format into its own destination. Final destinations must reflect implemented routes; avoid empty Explore/Profile pages.
- Suggested navigation architecture: Home/For You, Explore or topics (only when useful), central Post action, and selected discovery destinations. Post formats remain discoverable through filters or Explore. Do not blindly retain the old five post-type bottom tabs.
- Topic and sort controls remain accessible without a tall stacked toolbar.
- Respect bottom safe-area inset, browser chrome and Android keyboard; no clipped labels or obscured content.
- Composer opens as a lightweight mobile sheet/overlay with immediate text focus, not a long form page.

### Tablet: 768–1023 px

Centered feed around 620–700 px where space permits. Compact navigation; no oversized empty right column.

### Desktop: 1024+ px

Max shell ~1240 px; left rail ~200–240 px, central feed ~580–680 px, optional useful right rail ~240–280 px. Remove right rail before compressing feed. Sticky navigation must not obscure content. No fake community statistics.

**Review widths:** 320, 360, 390, 430, 768, 1024, 1440 px, plus zoom/font scaling. No horizontal overflow or unreachable controls.

## 4. Page and component behavior

### A. Home (`/`)

**Hierarchy:** CZ header → compact composer → For You/Latest and topic/format discovery → personalized feed. No marketing hero.

- Browsing and search work without signing in. Posting, commenting, voting, reacting and reporting require an authenticated account with verified email **or** phone.
- For You is the default recommendation feed; Latest is a chronological alternative. Format and topic filters are available without duplicating navigation.
- Feed includes **Regular**, **Confession**, **Petty Court**, **Am I Weird?**, **Hot Take**. Regular is default for creation; topics are optional and distinct from formats; no hashtags.
- Server-render initial results where appropriate; stable pagination/cursors, recoverable loading/error states and no fabricated production content.
- Empty state: small original mascot, concise copy and clear CTA.

### B. Post card (shared)

1. Author row: public pseudonym and chosen avatar **only for attributed posts**; anonymous posts display `Anonymous` and a generic avatar. Never expose private author identifiers or imply anonymous posts are connected to an account.
2. Subtle format chip, optional topic chip, relative time, 24-hour indicator if applicable, overflow actions.
3. Up to **256 user-perceived characters** of post text; preserve line breaks safely. Optional single image or GIF, max **2 MB**, with suitable alt-text handling.
4. Format-appropriate vote controls or reactions, comment count, share/report controls. No fake counts.
5. Post owner can manage their own content, including private archive/deletion controls; anonymous ownership never appears in public responses.

**Formats:**
- Regular: neutral warm accent; reactions and comments.
- Confession: coral/blush; `Same`, `Ouch`, `Wild` reactions (final reaction taxonomy subject to product-spec approval).
- Petty Court: muted amber; `Guilty` / `Not Guilty`.
- Am I Weird?: lavender; `Me Too` / `Just You`.
- Hot Take: muted teal; `Agree` / `Disagree`.

Keep one reusable card anatomy. One active reaction per user per post; one active vote per user per eligible poll. Selected states accessible; percentages and counts only from real data. `No votes yet` when empty. Do not make color the sole state signal.

### C. Post detail (`/post/[id]`)

- Return to prior filtered feed where possible; support direct links.
- Show full post, permitted interactions, comments and one-level replies; comments and replies max **256 grapheme clusters**.
- Expired 24-hour posts and archived/deleted posts are inaccessible publicly, including comments, reactions, votes and media. Their private archive remains available to the owner when archive was selected.
- Locked posts remain readable if otherwise public but disable writes with a reason.
- Deleted root with live replies: neutral placeholder only when retention rules permit.

### D. Lightweight composer

- Clicking homepage prompt opens a **compact, text-first** overlay/sheet. Do not create a separate exhaustive “Write a post” form page.
- Immediately usable textarea, prompt “What ruined your day today?”, **256-character** limit; count appears unobtrusively, prominently near limit.
- **Regular** format selected by default. Small icons/menu offer Confession, Petty Court, Am I Weird?, Hot Take; one optional topic. Never require choosing a format or topic to submit a regular post.
- Optional image/GIF picker: **one file**, maximum **2 MB**; preview, remove, clear errors, accessible description.
- **Identity toggle:** public pseudonym (default) or anonymous. Explain that anonymous means hidden from other users, not from protected moderation/ownership records.
- **Clock toggle:** optional **24-hour post**. On activation show `24h` state and a concise explanation; default expiry action is **archive privately**, with an option to delete permanently instead. Expiry choices must be understandable without a large form.
- Posts cannot be edited after publication; owners can archive or delete later, and manage 24-hour expiry action where allowed by product rules.
- Draft-discard confirmation only for non-empty drafts; preserve draft on recoverable errors. Clear submitting/loading state and accessible feedback.
- Enforce **three posts in any rolling 24-hour window** server-side; deleting a post does not refund the slot. Display actual retry time when limited.
- Account verification prompt appears only when an unauthenticated/unverified visitor tries to participate.

### E. Comments

- `Write a comment…`, maximum 256 grapheme clusters, clear Reply/Report/Delete controls.
- One reply level; no recursive deep thread. **Text and emoji only**, pending further approval of comment media.
- Owner attribution follows chosen privacy rules; no private author identifiers. Prevent duplicate rapid submissions and give recoverable errors.
- Empty: `No comments yet.` with optional invitation. Root deletion placeholder if live replies must remain.

### F. Profile, identity and settings

- Unique generated username editable during account creation; later rename at most once every rolling 30 days. Avatar from provided list or username initial.
- Public profile displays only content the user permits in public history; anonymous posts **never** appear on a public profile. Hiding history does not remove public attributed posts from feeds/search.
- Owner's private My Activity shows their own anonymous and archived content where retained.
- No real names, follower counts, badges or direct messages. No verification badge: email or phone verification is mandatory for participation.
- Settings: username/avatar, public-history visibility, verified contact/account management, privacy and deletion controls only if functional.

### G. Reporting and moderation

- Neutral report dialog with reason, optional detail, clear submit/error states; never sarcastic about harm.
- Permissive toward profanity, satire, harsh criticism and unpopular opinions; strict against spam, threats, doxxing, exploitation and serious targeted abuse.
- Protected moderator dashboard with queue, context, actions, confirmations and auditable decisions. Report counts alone must not automatically suppress unpopular opinions.

### H. Search and discovery

Search posts, public usernames and topics. Respect anonymous attribution, hidden profile history and expiry. Personalized For You uses user-interest signals plus freshness, quality and exploration; Latest remains available. No hashtags or fabricated trends.

### I. Rules, About, Privacy, Terms, system states

- Narrow readable legal/help pages consistent with actual authentication, media, logging, moderation, expiry and retention.
- 404: original mascot, concise joke and Home action. Network failure: Retry. No votes: honest zero state. Expired/removed: neutral unavailable message.
- Unverified or signed-out interaction: clear verify/sign-in path. Rate limits: real retry guidance. Loading: restrained skeletons.

## 5. Motion and interaction

- Hover/focus/pressed/disabled/loading for actionable controls. Destructive operations require confirmation where irreversible.
- Dialog focus trap, Escape where safe, focus restoration, screen-reader announcements; inline important errors.
- Transitions ~120–200 ms; respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. No autoplay GIF animation without appropriate user controls or motion consideration.
- No scroll jumps on filter changes, pagination, voting or comments. No decorative fake buttons.

## 6. Accessibility and performance

- WCAG AA target, keyboard navigation, visible focus, labeled controls, appropriate `aria-pressed`/`aria-selected`, readable results and error descriptions.
- Touch targets ~44×44 px where practical. Unicode, long strings, 200% zoom and large text without overflow.
- SSR Astro pages with selectively hydrated React islands; avoid full client-only app and unnecessary dependencies.
- Optimize media, avoid layout shifts, expensive fonts and heavy animations; suitable for modest Android devices and slow connections.

## 7. Development content and trust

- Short, varied, funny fixtures **only in development**, clearly marked in code. No fabricated production posts, reactions, votes, community metrics or endorsements.
- Don't repeat development warnings on every card. Security/moderation/privacy copy remains straightforward.
- Public pseudonyms and avatars are permitted for attributed posts; anonymous authorship must remain protected across APIs, search, feeds, media paths and recommendation signals.

## 9. Design quality and release gates

- [ ] Side-by-side mobile/desktop review establishes **at least 80% fidelity** to both approved reference images, with documented discrepancies.
- [ ] CZ-only header branding, one avatar/profile entry, no redundant “The zone” label.
- [ ] Feed starts high on mobile; compact composer, no form-like posting page.
- [ ] No duplicated post-type navigation; navigation and URL state agree.
- [ ] Five formats, Regular default, optional topic, 256-character text limits.
- [ ] Identity and 24-hour toggles are clear and accessible; private archive is the default expiry action.
- [ ] Media upload has visible 2 MB/one-file constraints and accessible preview.
- [ ] Public pseudonyms shown only when chosen; anonymous attribution never leaks.
- [ ] All public content is real; no fake votes, posts or statistics in production.
- [ ] No horizontal overflow at target widths; bottom nav and keyboard-safe composer work on Android.
- [ ] Functional interactions, accessibility, moderation, loading/error states and security rules verified.
- [ ] No migrations until product specification, database design, security matrix and test plan are approved.
